Shilun is the Lex family's truth-seeking sub-line: a workshop for paper retrieval and serious writing — papers and official documents alike.

## Three things

- **Retrieve** — paper search runs on the OpenAlex scholarly graph: exact DOI lookup, keyword search, honest citation counts and venues. Empty results always carry a reason.
- **Official docs** — document templates and norms are derived from hundreds of real central-government documents; every template links back to concrete samples, and under-sampled types are flagged.
- **Write** — citation lists export as GB/T 7714 or APA; outline sections name their evidence; generated citations are checked one by one and stripped if they fail to resolve.

## Resolvable citation

Trust in serious writing rests on verifiability: every citation Shilun generates must resolve to a real retrieval hit or a real corpus entry.
