Zhesi is the Lex family's contemplative sub-line — a long-term workshop for your worldview. It does not sell philosophy as content; it lets the history of philosophy serve as your reference frame and sparring partner, and the deliverable is your own positions.

## Three loops

- **Ask** — plain language in, philosophical coordinates out. Concept clarifications anchor to cited encyclopedia entries; every stance question carries the distribution of professional philosophers' positions.
- **Debate** — after you commit, Zhesi finds the strongest opposing argument in the tradition. Every objection carries a clickable citation.
- **Map** — six-axis projection and thought neighbors: individual vs. universal, rational vs. empirical, transcendent vs. material. Position changes settle into annals of thought; history is never overwritten.

## Reference frame and boundaries

The reference frame assembles four community-maintained sources (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Philosophy Atlas, Wikidata, PhilPapers Surveys) with no self-built extraction. The personal layer — stance cards, tensions, annals — stays in private storage with authorized, audited access; private deployment available.
