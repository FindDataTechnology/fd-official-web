Vertical packs are the industry entry points of the Constellation (萬星) line:
each pack is one skill that turns the platform's chat surface into a
domain-workflow assistant — not a generic chatbot with a system prompt, but a
curated workflow with data tools behind it.

## The four packs

- **legal-contract-workflow** — 法律·合同: contract drafting and review with
  the LawBench MCP behind it.
- **legal-case-workflow** — 法律·案件: legal retrieval across the law corpus.
- **stock-research-workflow** — 数据·股票: stock research over the Open-Data
  MCP and Chinese financial reports.
- **china-macro-brief-workflow** — 数据·宏观: China macro briefings over the
  same data spine.

Packs are maintained as open assets
([fd-vertical-packs](https://github.com/FindDataTechnology/fd-vertical-packs))
and installed from the platform Store; the agent catalog wires each pack to
its model endpoint and data tools.
