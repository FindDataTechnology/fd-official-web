Juchuang is the Lex family's fiction sub-line: a script workshop for the AI era. It spends its effort upstream — structure, conflict, stance; the picture is only the last skin.

## Three things

- **Script** — projects, characters, scenes, and lines are all structured data: character cards lock appearance sets, scenes carry conflict metadata, version snapshots roll back anytime, and the whole tree exports as JSON.
- **Core** — connects the Zhesi workshop: real tensions between your own positions become a script's central conflict, and stance cards grow into characters. Writes standalone when Zhesi is away; re-link restores in one click.
- **Material** — scenes break into storyboards (shot size, visual, action, line, duration), then into image, video, and narrative prompts; prompts across shots automatically carry the same appearance set and character ID.

## Relationship with Zhesi

Zhesi clarifies your stance on the philosophical axes; Juchuang puts the tension between stances on stage — AI generation is capped by the script beneath it.
