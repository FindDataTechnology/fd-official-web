Our products have names now. Five of them.

寻数·壹座 Base. 寻数·识律 Lex. 寻数·柏讯 Wire. 寻数·谦面 Facet. 寻数·萬星 Constellation.

Say the Chinese names out loud and listen for the numbers: one, ten, hundred,
thousand, ten thousand. Yizuo (壹座) and Wanxing (萬星) write their numbers
openly in formal script — the base and the stars. Shilü (识律), Baixun (柏讯),
and Qianmian (谦面) hide theirs in plain speech. The bigger the number, the
wider the network: from the single execution base every project runs on, to a
myriad of agents — each person's own.

## Base

The execution base — every FindData project runs on this AI harness platform,
across web, mini-program, and desktop, with private deployment.

## Lex

Law, made legible — LawBench contract intelligence, fine-tuned legal models,
and the legal corpus.

## Wire

Let AI read the world for you — one open-data ontology, MCP data services, and
the crawl platform behind them.

## Facet

A thousand facets — everyone assembles their own text-and-data AI workflows,
starting with the DAAS platform.

## Constellation

People connected through their agents — a growing line; FindDataRouter, the AI
API gateway, is its first star.

Old product links redirect to the new five-line catalog. One seat, a myriad of
stars — 壹座之上，萬星闪耀。
